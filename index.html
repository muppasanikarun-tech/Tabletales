The founder's build plan

Earn the right to build the next thing.

Four focused 60-day phases. One clear job at a time. The dates below are planned windows, not a claim that these features are already live.

01
28 Jan – 28 Mar 2027 · Days 1–60

Make the visit effortless

Start with the guest journey and prove it works inside a real venue.

  • Venue + table QR check-in
  • Menu, cart and order submission
  • Bill estimate and order status
  • Waiter / water / glass requests
  • Pilot with real staff workflows
Milestone · Reliable guest MVP
02
29 Mar – 27 May 2027 · Days 61–120

Give the night a memory

Introduce the venue's own living guestbook and repeat-visit loop.

  • My Cafe / Brewery / Venue Story
  • Photo + caption submission
  • Moderated TV Story Wall
  • Likes, leaderboard and weekly coupon
  • Consent, reporting and anti-abuse rules
Milestone · Story Wall pilot
03
28 May – 26 Jul 2027 · Days 121–180

Turn moments into return visits

Help venues bring people back with measurable, permission-based marketing.

  • Guest opt-in and loyalty basics
  • Campaigns and reward redemption
  • Story-to-reel workflow prototype
  • Venue engagement dashboard
  • Measure repeat visits and conversions
Milestone · Measurable engagement
04
27 Jul – 24 Sep 2027 · Days 181–240

Connect the operation

Expand POS and business workflows based on what pilot venues actually need.

  • POS / KDS integration priorities
  • Staff assignment and order routing
  • Billing and payment integration
  • Owner reports and multi-outlet needs
  • Pricing, onboarding and support model
Milestone · Operational integrations
